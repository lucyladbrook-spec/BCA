For teachers

Running Nature Explorers

Nature Explorers is a pupil-facing draft to support Bengeo's climate action work, which the School Business Manager leads. Anything here is a proposal until she agrees it. This page covers logging, the Energy Garden, photos, safeguarding and curriculum links.

Class logbook

Saved only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. At the end of each week, copy the log into the shared eco spreadsheet, then clear it. Logs never include pupil names.

Updating the Energy Garden

Only the School Business Manager decides each week's symbol, from the meter readings. Compare each week with the same week last year. In a very cold week she can choose “About the same” so the weather doesn't count against the school. Pupils only ever see the symbol, never the figures.

Make this week's line

Choose the week and the symbol. Then copy the line into DATA.energyGarden.weeks in the file and republish.

Keeping it honest

Every project, counter and story shows its real status: Idea (suggested, not yet agreed) Planned Under way Done. Counters stay at “Not measured yet” until a real figure is cleared to share. Discovery stories only appear when a class has really done the project. Until then, pupils see an invitation.

Safeguarding

Photos

  • Only use images of pupils with consent on file. Check the school's photo-consent list first.
  • Prefer hands, backs and nature close-ups to faces.
  • Never pair a photo with a pupil's name, and remove names from work in shot.
  • Strip location data (EXIF) before adding files.

Words and data

  • No pupil names, home addresses or personal details anywhere on the site, in logs or on idea cards.
  • Travel surveys and route maps start from public landmarks, never from homes.
  • Question-wall entries are anonymous. Credit them to a class or year group only.
  • The site has no sign-ins, forms that send data, or links to outside sites for pupils.

Outdoors

  • Pupils explore with an adult nearby and stay within agreed boundaries.
  • Gloves for anything to do with bins. Hand-washing after soil, minibeasts and waste.
  • Leave wildlife where it's found, and never touch fungi or berries.
  • Follow the school's outdoor-learning risk assessment.

Sharing this site

  • Anyone with the link can open this site, including this page. Agree with SLT who the link goes to, and never put pupil names or school data in it.
  • Ask the School Business Manager to clear energy figures before they're shown to pupils.

Photo slots

Each slot has a code. To fill one, save the photo next to this file as photos/CODE.jpg (for example photos/EX-01.jpg), then add that path as the slot's src in the DATA block, along with alt text. Empty slots show “Photo coming soon”. For now every slot has a temporary stock photo from Unsplash (free under the Unsplash Licence). Pupils don't see a label, so swap each one for a real school photo when you can.

The grounds map

The map is a sketch layout, not our real grounds yet. To match Bengeo, change each zone's x, y, w and h in DATA.zones, or paste a hand-traced d path. The wobbly hand-drawn edges are added automatically. Nature is a suggested extra area, on top of the four in the sustainability audit (energy, waste, buying and travel). Trees and paths live in DATA.mapDecor. Set each zone's status as the work moves on.
